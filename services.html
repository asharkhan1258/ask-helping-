What we do, plainly:

Ask Helping Hand is a free information and referral service. We are not a government agency and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any government program, the U.S. government, HUD, USDA, HHS, the Department of Education, or any state agency.

We do not provide money, financial aid, food, or debt services, and we do not submit applications on your behalf. We do not pay rent, utility bills, insurance premiums, or medical bills, and we do not issue checks, gift cards, loans, or financial aid. We help you find programs you can apply to directly — most of them for free.

AW Consulting LLC DBA askhelpinghand.com is a privately held company, funded by its members. We are not a nonprofit or charity. We are not a lender, bank, money transmitter, debt-relief or credit-counseling provider, or food establishment. We never sell your personal information.
