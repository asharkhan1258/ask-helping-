Honest answers.

Common questions about the referral service.

Is the call free?

Yes. This demo site's concept is a free referral service; no payment is required to ask for a referral.

Are you a government agency?

No. A referral service is not the same as a government agency or benefits provider.

Do you pay my rent or utility bills?

No. Benefits and payments, where available, come from the relevant program. A referral service only helps identify where to apply.

Will you submit forms for me?

No. You complete and submit applications directly to the appropriate program.

Is assistance guaranteed?

No. Eligibility, availability and approval are determined by each program.

Can I call instead of filling out a form?

Yes. Call (844) 910-6769 during the listed business hours.
