Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AW Consulting LLC DBA askhelpinghand.com (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website or request a callback for information and referrals.

1. Information We Collect

When you call us or submit a callback request, we collect:

  • Contact information (name, email, phone, location)
  • Household information (size, composition)
  • Employment and income information (for matching to programs)
  • Description of your situation and the topics you're seeking referrals about

We may automatically collect:

  • Browser type, device, and operating system
  • IP address (used for security and abuse prevention)
  • Pages visited on our site

2. How We Use Information

  • To identify the right federal programs, state agencies, and local resources to refer you to
  • To call you back about your request
  • To follow up and confirm you got through to the programs we referred
  • To improve our service
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. How We Share Information

We never sell or rent your personal information. We do not share it with insurance partners, lead-gen networks, marketing companies, or any third party for their own use.

Submissions from our contact form are stored in a private Google Sheets workspace we use to track callback requests internally. Google Workspace is our infrastructure provider only — they do not use your information for advertising or any other purpose, and access is restricted to our team.

The only other situations in which we may disclose information are:

  • For legal compliance — if required by law, court order, or a government request directed to us.

Important: Government agencies and programs (211, LIHEAP, USDA, HUD, SNAP, Medicaid, etc.) that we refer you to are contacted by you directly. We do not transmit your information to those programs on your behalf. You will share information with them yourself, on their terms.

4. SMS / Phone Communications

If you opt in to SMS or phone communications, message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. We will only contact you regarding your referral request — we do not send marketing messages.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as needed to address your request and to comply with legal obligations. The length of time depends on the nature of your inquiry. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see “Your Rights” below).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of communications at any time

To exercise these rights, email privacy@askhelpinghand.com.

7. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA and CPRA, including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to delete personal information. We do not sell personal information.

8. Children

Our service is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and at rest. No system is 100% secure, but we work hard to safeguard your data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will reflect any changes.

What we do, plainly:

Ask Helping Hand is a free information and referral service. We are not a government agency and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any government program, the U.S. government, HUD, USDA, HHS, the Department of Education, or any state agency.

We do not provide money, financial aid, food, or debt services, and we do not submit applications on your behalf. We do not pay rent, utility bills, insurance premiums, or medical bills, and we do not issue checks, gift cards, loans, or financial aid. We help you find programs you can apply to directly — most of them for free.

AW Consulting LLC DBA askhelpinghand.com is a privately held company, funded by its members. We are not a nonprofit or charity. We are not a lender, bank, money transmitter, debt-relief or credit-counseling provider, or food establishment. We never sell your personal information.
