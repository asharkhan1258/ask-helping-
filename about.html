Help should feel human.

Ask Helping Hand runs a free information and referral service for people looking for help with rent, utilities, food, healthcare, financial benefits, and education or job training.

What we are

We help you find the right place to call.

Most assistance websites are designed to capture your information and sell it. Forms get submitted into a void. Auto-responses promise help that never comes. People in genuine hardship get treated like marketing leads.

We do it differently. A real team member picks up the phone, listens, and tells you which federal programs, state agencies, local food banks, and verified hotlines fit your situation — along with the questions to ask when you call them.

We don't pay your bills, issue checks or gift cards, handle food, manage debt, or submit forms for you. You apply directly with each program.

What we do

  • Plain-language information
  • Direct referrals to relevant federal programs
  • Connections to state agencies and local food banks
  • Verified crisis and assistance hotlines
  • Questions to ask when contacting programs
  • You apply directly with each program

Our Team

Real people.

The people who answer the phone are trained on the federal programs (SNAP, LIHEAP, Section 8, Medicaid, ACA, FAFSA, and others), the local food bank networks, and the verified hotlines we refer to.

Our team works real business hours from our Illinois office — no offshore call center, no gig-worker scripts, no commissions tied to upsells. They're paid to help you find the right call to make.
